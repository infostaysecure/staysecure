# StaySecure Starter Courses — Avatar Video Production

## Instructor
One consistent gender-neutral illustrated guide: approachable, professional, navy clothing, green StaySecure shield-and-lock lapel mark, blue/green studio. Calm, clear, inclusive voice. Present the avatar as a branded educational presenter, not a real human expert.

## Production requirements
- One 2–4 minute video per module; course duration also includes reading and activities.
- 16:9 landscape, 1080p, clean audio, captions (WebVTT or SRT), transcript, and visual recap.
- Use fictional examples. Never show real credentials, personal data, or live malicious links.
- Host approved MP4s on an approved video host/CDN. Add video URLs only after review.
- No placeholder footage should be presented as a finished lesson.

## Cybersecurity Fundamentals — seven scripts
1. Understanding Cybersecurity — confidentiality, integrity, availability, layered security.
2. Passwords and Authentication — unique passwords, managers, MFA, passkeys, recovery codes.
3. Device Security — updates, locks, trusted apps, permissions, backups.
4. Wi-Fi and Networks — router defaults, WPA2/WPA3, public Wi-Fi, VPN limitations.
5. Safe Browsing and Social Engineering — urgency, impersonation, sender/context checks, independent verification.
6. Protecting Personal Information — digital footprint, app permissions, privacy settings.
7. Your Personal Security Action Plan — sustainable routine and compromise response.

## Phishing & Online Scams — seven scripts
1. Understanding Phishing — channels, goals, impersonation, urgency.
2. Email Scams and Sender Checks — full sender address, context, unexpected requests.
3. Text, Phone, and Impersonation Scams — smishing, spoofing, one-time-code safety.
4. Links, Attachments, and QR Codes — known bookmarks, official apps, unexpected files.
5. Social Engineering and Payment Scams — urgency, secrecy, authority, unusual payments.
6. Reporting and Recovering — response based on what was clicked or disclosed.
7. Your Scam-Spotting Playbook — Stop, Think, Verify, Report, Recover.

## Assessment and shield policy
Each course has five final questions. Proposed passing score: 80% (4/5). Learners must complete all seven modules and pass the final assessment to earn the course-specific shield. Persistent awards must be verified server-side; do not award from browser-only state.

## Video status
Scripts are authored in course-curriculum.js. Finished talking-avatar video files are not included. Add hosted video URLs only after rendered videos, captions, and transcripts have been reviewed.
